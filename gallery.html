Why categories help

A premium gallery should guide people, not just show them a pile of images.

A category-first structure makes the gallery easier to browse, easier to expand, and more useful during the decision-making process. It also gives Tailored room to keep adding completed work without making the page feel cluttered.

More organized

Each project type has its own home instead of competing in one mixed gallery feed.

More scalable

New completed work can be added into the right category as the portfolio grows.

More persuasive

Clients can quickly compare the visual direction most relevant to their own space.

Ready to move from browsing to planning?

Use the category that feels closest to your project as the starting point.

Once you know which category best reflects your taste or space type, bring that into the consultation and Tailored can shape the design direction from there.
